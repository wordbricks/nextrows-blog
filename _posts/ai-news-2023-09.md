![Timeline of September 2023 AI news](/assets/blog/ai-news-2023-09-timeline.svg)

September ended the era of the text-only chatbot. The flagship assistant learned to look and to talk, and the money behind its biggest rival got very large.

## DALL-E 3 lands inside ChatGPT (September 20)

OpenAI unveiled DALL-E 3, built natively on ChatGPT so the chatbot itself writes the detailed prompts, and promised rollout to Plus and Enterprise customers in early October. OpenAI also said creators could opt their work out of future training, an early concession in the growing fight over training data.

## ChatGPT learns to see, hear and speak (September 25)

Five days later, OpenAI gave Plus and Enterprise users voice conversations and image input: hold a back-and-forth spoken conversation, or snap a photo of a broken bike and discuss how to fix it. Rolling out over two weeks, the update turned the assistant from a text box into something closer to a companion.

## Amazon commits up to $4 billion to Anthropic (September 25)

Amazon agreed to invest up to $4 billion in Anthropic, which named AWS its primary cloud provider and committed to training future models on Amazon's Trainium and Inferentia chips. Frontier AI development had become an infrastructure alliance, with the cloud giants picking their horses.

## Why this month matters if you work with web data

September made AI something you talk to and show things to. The inputs and outputs are still data, and teams that need that data clean, current and structured are who NextRows builds for.
