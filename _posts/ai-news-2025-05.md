Three days in late May compressed what felt like a season of news: video learned to speak, AI got a design studio, and Anthropic planted its flag at the frontier.

## Google I/O: Veo 3 generates video with sound (May 20)

At Google I/O, the company unveiled Veo 3, the first of its video models to generate audio alongside the picture - dialogue, sound effects and ambient noise, timed to the scene. It launched inside Flow, a new AI filmmaking tool, alongside Imagen 4 for stills. Silent AI video ended this month; the demo clips, from fake nature documentaries to street interviews, spread everywhere within hours.

## Jony Ive's io joins OpenAI (May 21)

A day later, Sam Altman and Jony Ive published a joint letter: io, the device company Ive built with a team of former Apple designers, would merge with OpenAI in an all-equity deal reported at about $6.5 billion. Ive and his firm LoveFrom stay independent but take on deep design and creative responsibility across OpenAI. The stated goal is a family of devices built for the AI era - a bet that the chat window is not the final form of this technology.

## Anthropic launches Claude 4 (May 22)

Anthropic answered with Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4. Both are hybrid reasoning models that can call tools - search, code execution - in the middle of extended thinking, and Opus 4 was billed as the world's best coding model, capable of sustained, hours-long autonomous work on complex tasks. Claude Code, previewed in February, went generally available the same day.
