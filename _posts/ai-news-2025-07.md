July made the agent era concrete: a new flagship from xAI, a ChatGPT that operates its own computer, and a national strategy for the technology from Washington.

## xAI ships Grok 4 (July 9)

xAI launched Grok 4, its new flagship, with native tool use and real-time search built in, available to SuperGrok and Premium+ subscribers and through the API. Alongside it came Grok 4 Heavy - a version that spawns multiple agents to work a problem in parallel - under a new top subscription tier. xAI, founded in 2023, now had a seat at the frontier table.

## ChatGPT gains an agent of its own (July 17)

OpenAI unified its agent work into a single product: ChatGPT agent. It merges Operator's ability to drive a browser with deep research's ability to synthesize, and adds its own virtual computer - it can run code, use a terminal, and produce editable spreadsheets and slide decks. It asks permission before consequential actions like purchases or email. The chatbot that once answered questions now takes assignments.

## The White House releases America's AI Action Plan (July 23)

The White House published "Winning the AI Race: America's AI Action Plan," a national strategy with more than 90 federal policy actions across three pillars: accelerating AI innovation, building American AI infrastructure, and leading in international AI diplomacy and security. Three executive orders accompanied it the same day. AI policy moved from hearings and frameworks into a build-first national program.
