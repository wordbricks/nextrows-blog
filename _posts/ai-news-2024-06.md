June showed AI becoming the center of the technology industry: the largest device maker, the largest chip company and a leading model lab all made major moves within ten days.

## Apple introduces Apple Intelligence (June 10)

Apple announced Apple Intelligence, a personal intelligence system for iPhone, iPad and Mac, built into iOS 18, iPadOS 18 and macOS Sequoia. It combines generative models with personal context and runs on-device where possible, with Private Cloud Compute extending to larger server-based models on Apple silicon servers.

## Nvidia becomes the world's most valuable company (June 18)

Reuters reported that Nvidia passed Microsoft to become the world's most valuable company, with a market capitalization of about $3.335 trillion after its shares rose 3.5% to $135.58. Microsoft stood at $3.317 trillion. Reuters tied the rise to demand for Nvidia's high-end processors in the race to build AI.

## Anthropic releases Claude 3.5 Sonnet (June 20)

Anthropic launched Claude 3.5 Sonnet, the first model in its 3.5 family. It outperformed Claude 3 Opus on a wide range of evaluations at twice the speed and the cost of the mid-tier Claude 3 Sonnet. It was free on Claude.ai, and in the API it cost $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, with a 200K token context window.
