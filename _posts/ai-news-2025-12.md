December offered no holiday pause: Europe's open champion shipped its biggest model, OpenAI fired back at the frontier, and Google made its newest model the fast default.

## Mistral 3: open weights from Europe (December 2)

Mistral AI released Mistral 3, a family led by Mistral Large 3 - a mixture-of-experts model with 41 billion active parameters across 675 billion total, released under Apache 2.0. Alongside it came Ministral 3 in 3B, 8B and 14B sizes, tuned for edge deployment. In a year of record closed-model spending, Europe's flagship lab doubled down on open weights.

## OpenAI launches GPT-5.2 for knowledge work (December 11)

OpenAI answered Gemini 3 in under a month with GPT-5.2, in Instant, Thinking and Pro variants, pitched squarely at professional knowledge work: spreadsheets, presentations, long documents, code. On GDPval, which scores well-specified tasks across 44 occupations, GPT-5.2 Thinking beats or ties industry professionals on 70.9% of comparisons - the first time an OpenAI model crossed that line. The year-end sprint had a clear message: the frontier moves in weeks now, not seasons.

## Gemini 3 Flash makes frontier the default (December 17)

Google finished the year with Gemini 3 Flash: Gemini 3's reasoning at Flash-tier speed and cost, with a 78% score on SWE-bench Verified that beats several larger models. More importantly, it replaced the previous Flash as the default model in the Gemini app - so the frontier, or something near it, is now what hundreds of millions of people get without asking.

## Why this month matters if you work with web data

December's lesson closes the year's arc: models got cheaper, faster and more open, and none of that removes the need for good input. Every agent, app and default model still depends on reading the web accurately.
