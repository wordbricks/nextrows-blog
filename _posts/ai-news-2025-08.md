August delivered the year's most-watched model launch - and, two days before it, a quieter release that may matter just as much: OpenAI gave weights away again.

## OpenAI releases gpt-oss, its first open weights since GPT-2 (August 5)

OpenAI published gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b under the Apache 2.0 license - its first open-weight language models since GPT-2 in 2019. The larger model rivals o4-mini on reasoning benchmarks; the smaller one runs on a laptop with 16 GB of memory. Both do chain-of-thought, tool use and agentic work, free to download, fine-tune and run anywhere. The company that closed its models opened the door again.

## GPT-5 arrives for everyone (August 7)

GPT-5 launched as one system instead of a model picker: a fast model for most questions, a deeper reasoning model for harder ones, and a real-time router that decides which to use. It rolled out to all signed-in ChatGPT users, including the free tier, with state-of-the-art results in coding, writing and health, and large reductions in hallucinations. The router itself was the tell: knowing how hard to think had become the product.

## 'Nano banana' is revealed as Gemini 2.5 Flash Image (August 26)

For weeks, an anonymous model codenamed "nano banana" had topped image-editing leaderboards. Google confirmed it was Gemini 2.5 Flash Image: an editor that keeps a character consistent across scenes, applies targeted natural-language edits, and fuses multiple images into one. The episode doubled as a lesson in modern launches - the capability won the internet before the brand did.
