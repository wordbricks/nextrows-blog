![Timeline of September 2026 AI news](/assets/blog/ai-news-2026-09-timeline.svg)

September was the month consumer agents stopped being demos and started being products.

## Meta launches Muse, a consumer agent (September 8)

Meta launched Muse in the US through a dedicated app and WhatsApp: an agent that can access a user's other apps to do things like send emails or make payments on their behalf, per Reuters. The platform with billions of users shipped the idea that an assistant should act inside your apps - with the user's delegation, not just conversation.

## OpenAI puts a Data agent in ChatGPT (September 10 and 29)

OpenAI brought its internal Data agent to ChatGPT Business, Enterprise and Edu: ask in natural language and get governed analytics over connected warehouses like Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks and ClickHouse - the same agent OpenAI said its own employees had used for over a year. Weeks later, DevDay 2026 (September 29) showed where this leads: Dots, always-on agents that run even when you are not at your desk; an Agents API with computer use; and GPT-6.1 Sol, an upgrade to the paid GPT-6 line ahead of its free-tier arrival in October. Agents graduated from copilots to digital coworkers.

## Siri AI reaches the iPhone with iOS 27 (September 14)

Apple's fall software lineup - which Apple brands its "2027 releases," including iOS 27 - shipped on September 14 (CNBC), and with it Siri AI began rolling out in beta, in English first, with more languages promised for the next month. The rebuilt assistant understands personal context and takes actions in and across apps. Announced in June, it was now in hundreds of millions of pockets - with reporting that iOS 27 also lets users choose third-party chatbots like Claude or Gemini for Apple Intelligence. The assistant wars moved to the lock screen.

## Why this month matters if you work with web data

The pattern across September: agents act on structured, governed data. A Data agent over Snowflake and a personal agent over your apps are the same idea - intelligence needs clean inputs. That is exactly the layer where NextRows operates: describe the table, get validated rows.
