![Timeline of November 2023 AI news](/assets/blog/ai-news-2023-11-timeline.svg)

November gave the industry its busiest month: a new chatbot from a new lab, a platform shift from the leader, and a governance crisis that nearly dissolved the most important company in AI over a single weekend.

## xAI announces Grok (November 4)

Elon Musk's xAI introduced Grok, its first chatbot, with real-time access to posts on X and a stated willingness to answer "spicy questions" other assistants refuse. Early access went to a limited group of US users. Four months after the company's founding, the frontier had a new entrant with a built-in distribution channel.

## DevDay: GPT-4 Turbo and custom GPTs (November 6)

At its first developer conference, OpenAI announced GPT-4 Turbo with a 128,000-token context window and lower prices, plus GPTs - custom versions of ChatGPT that anyone can build without code - and a coming store to share them. The chatbot was becoming a platform, and the platform was getting cheaper to build on.

## OpenAI fires Sam Altman, then reinstates him (November 17)

OpenAI's board removed Altman as CEO, saying he had not been consistently candid in his communications. Within five days, after nearly all of the company's roughly 770 employees signed a letter threatening to leave and Microsoft offered to hire the team, the board reversed course and Altman returned under a new initial board. The episode exposed how unusual OpenAI's structure was - and how much of the industry depended on it holding together.

## Why this month matters if you work with web data

November showed how fast the industry's center of gravity can move. Teams that own their data pipelines, rather than renting a single vendor's roadmap, keep moving through shocks like this - one reason NextRows focuses on portable, structured output.
