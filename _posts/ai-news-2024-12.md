December ended the year by lifting the ceiling on price, capability and measurement at the same time.

## OpenAI launches ChatGPT Pro (December 5)

OpenAI added ChatGPT Pro, a $200 monthly plan with unlimited access to OpenAI o1, o1-mini, GPT-4o and Advanced Voice. It also includes o1 pro mode, a version of o1 that uses more compute to think harder on difficult problems.

## Google introduces Gemini 2.0 (December 11)

Google introduced Gemini 2.0, a model family for what it called the agentic era, meaning models that understand more about the world, think multiple steps ahead and take action with supervision. The first release was an experimental Gemini 2.0 Flash, which Google said outperformed 1.5 Pro on key benchmarks at twice the speed and added multimodal output. Google also launched a Deep Research feature in Gemini.

## OpenAI previews o3 (December 20)

OpenAI announced o3 and o3-mini, its next reasoning models, on the final day of its 12 days of OpenAI event. Ars Technica reported that OpenAI was not releasing them yet and would open them first to safety researchers, with o3-mini planned for late January. The ARC Prize reported that o3 scored 75.7% on the ARC-AGI semi-private evaluation within its public leaderboard compute limit, and 87.5% with a high-compute configuration. ARC Prize later noted that the version OpenAI eventually released differed from the one it tested.
